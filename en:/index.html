For your holiday rental, we offer this charming little gîte, a fisherman's cottage typical of the region, located just a stone's throw from Lézardrieux and the sea. It can accommodate up to two people.

🌿 Peace & Nature

Situated on the higher ground of the town, the property features a garden tucked away from the road. The terrace is perfect for enjoying the sunshine, whether during a meal or while taking a nap, a favorite holiday pastime. It is a unique spot by today’s standards, as you won’t find a television or Wi-Fi here... though there are books... and 5G reception is excellent. It offers a peaceful setting just steps from the Trieux River, an ideal place to recharge and enjoy the private garden.

⚓ Ideal Location in Côtes d'Armor

However, our vacation rental, Le Gîte du Pêcheur, is just a ten-minute walk from Lézardrieux and its town center, and thus its shops: a bakery, a newsagent/tobacconist, a wine shop, and a convenience store. A market is also held there every Friday, offering a chance to sample local products and stock up on fresh fruit and vegetables, freshly caught fish, and more.
The GR34, also known as the "Sentier des douaniers"a>, runs close to our house. It winds along the Brittany coast for some two thousand kilometers, stretching from the port of Saint-Nazaire to Mont Saint-Michel.
The marina, which can accommodate five hundred boats, is about a fifteen-minute walk away
L’Arcouest, where you can catch the shuttle boats to the island of Bréhat, is a fifteen-minute drive away, while Paimpol is just a ten-minute drive.

🚶 Scenic Walks & Sightseeing

From coastal hikes along the GR34 trail to iconic islands and geological wonders, explore the top destinations near Lézardrieux.

🏝️ Bréhat Island (Île de Bréhat)

📍 L'Arcouest ferry terminal (Ploubazlanec)
🚗 15 min drive + 10 min boat crossing

Known as the "Island of Flowers", car-free Bréhat can be explored on foot or by bicycle. The southern island features vibrant gardens and cafés, while the northern part offers dramatic, wild coastal landscapes.

🌾 Sillon de Talbert Spit

📍 Pleubian — 🚗 12 km (15 min drive)

A unique geological marvel in Europe: a 3.2 km narrow spit of pebbles extending straight into the sea. An exceptional nature reserve perfect for bird watching and bracing coastal walks. Watch the rising tide!

🪨 The Pink Granite Coast

📍 Trégastel & Ploumanac'h — 🚗 40 km (45 min drive)

Famous for its giant pink granite rock formations sculpted by the sea and wind. A must-see walk along the famous GR34 coastal path. Local tip: Visit Renote Peninsula in Trégastel at high tide!

🌊 The Wild Peninsula (Presqu'île Sauvage)

📍 Around Lézardrieux & Pleubian

Far less busy than the Pink Granite Coast, this preserved peninsula offers peaceful scenic walks off the beaten track through authentic Breton landscapes.

🌊 The Abyss of Plougrescant (Le Gouffre)

📍 Plougrescant — 🚗 20 km (25 min drive)

A dramatic rocky chasm carved into the coastline where sea waves crash with formidable power. Nearby stands the famous picturesque cottage wedged between two massive granite boulders.

⛪ Saint-Maudez Chapel in Kermouster

📍 Kermouster Hamlet, Lézardrieux
🚗 5 km (7 min drive / reachable on foot via GR34)

A historic 12th-century chapel perched above the Trieux estuary, offering breathtaking views of Bréhat Island. Guided tours available in July and August (2 PM to 7 PM, closed Mondays).

🌊 The Trieux Estuary & Trégor Country

Located in Lézardrieux, at the gateway to the Wild Peninsula, our cottage sits right along the Trieux river. The town boasts the region's only deep-water harbor, naturally sheltered from coastal winds and storms.

🌉 Lézardrieux Bridge & Tidal Bay

📍 Lézardrieux — 🚶 10 min walk from the cottage

Connecting the historic Trégor and Goëlo regions, the landmark suspension bridge overlooks a magnificent 150-hectare maritime bay. Famed for oyster farming, the estuary is a protected haven for migratory seabirds (brent geese, shelducks, plovers) visible along the shore at low tide.

⚓ The Trieux Valley & Jaudy Estuary

📍 Between Lézardrieux, Pontrieux, and Tréguier

Upstream towards Pontrieux, the Trieux river flows through steep, wooded valleys renowned for trout and salmon fishing. Nearby towards Tréguier, the wild Jaudy estuary forms a European protected nature reserve for coastal wildlife.

🌉 The Historic Canada Bridge in Tréguier

📍 Between Tréguier & Trédarzec — 🚗 10 km (12 min drive)

Spanning the Jaudy river between Tréguier and Trédarzec, Canada Bridge was originally built in 1833 as Brittany's first suspension bridge. Rebuilt with double concrete arches in 1954, it offers stunning views of Tréguier's historic cathedral town.

The Four Seasons in Côtes-d'Armor

Throughout the year, the light and landscapes of the Wild Peninsula constantly change.
Enjoy pleasant summer temperatures away from heatwaves, and breathtaking coastal scenery in every season.
